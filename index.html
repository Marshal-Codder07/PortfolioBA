01The Problem

Applying for a firearms licence in South Australia has traditionally been paper-based: a PD303 form, an in-person lodgement, physical transfer to Firearms Branch, and manual re-entry into the register.

2×

Double data entry

Every application is written by the applicant and re-typed by staff — double effort and transcription errors.

!

Incomplete applications

A paper form can't stop someone submitting without a key document. Each gap triggers a slow rework loop.

?

No visibility

Applicants can't see where their application is, so they call and email — taking officer time away from processing.

✉

Slow paper communication

Postal transit, outcome letters and posted licence cards add weeks.

Problem statement: "Paper-based firearms licence applications create double handling, rework and long waiting times for applicants and Firearms Branch, because the current process has no validation at lodgement, no digital data flow, and no self-service status tracking."

05Root Cause Analysis

5 Whys on the main problem: "Why does it take ~5 months to receive a licence?"

Why?Applications wait weeks in a queue before assessment starts.
Why?Each application needs manual handling: transit, completeness checking and re-typing.
Why?Applications arrive on paper, often incomplete.
Why?A paper form cannot validate information at the point of lodgement.
Root causeThe process relies on unvalidated paper capture with no digital data flow between the applicant and the register.

12User Stories

Format: As a [user], I want [goal], so that [benefit], with acceptance criteria in Given / When / Then.

WORKED EXAMPLE · US-01 · APPLY ONLINE
As an applicant, I want to apply for a firearms licence online, so that I don't have to complete a paper form and visit a service point.
Linked: BR-01, BR-02, BR-03, BR-04Priority: MustEstimate: 8 points
  1. Given I am logged in, when I complete all mandatory fields, upload documents, pay and submit, then my application is lodged and I see a confirmation screen.
  2. Given a mandatory field or document is missing, when I click Submit, then the missing items are highlighted and the application is not submitted.
  3. Given my payment fails, when I submit, then I see an error and my form data is kept so I can try again.
  4. Given I save and leave part-way, when I log back in, then my draft is restored.

Definition of done: acceptance criteria pass in UAT · accessibility check passed · Registry and applicant representatives have signed off.

14What I Learned

Most delay is waiting, not working

Queue time and rework loops — not the assessment — drive time to licence.

Validation at the start saves weeks later

One rule — "you can't submit without your ID document" — removes the most common rework loop.

Automation should stop where judgement starts

In a regulated public-safety service, automate the paperwork and keep decisions with trained people.

Change impact matters as much as the system

Digital exclusion and go-live backlogs are the biggest risks — both need a plan before launch.
